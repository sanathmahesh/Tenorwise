# Assumptions

Every simplifying assumption the model makes, where it is used, and its likely
effect. Anything whose result appears in the interface is labelled there too.
Effects described as "not measured" have not been quantified; none of these
statements is a measurement unless it says so.

| ID | Assumption (plain English) | Where used | Likely effect | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Time is counted in whole months: a cash flow n months away is n/12 years away. There are no day-count conventions, holidays or settlement delays. | Everywhere (`tenorwise/curve/bootstrap.py`; later all instruments) | Cash-flow timing differs from real market conventions by days. This gives small price differences against market quotes (not measured). | 1 |
| A-02 | FRED's constant-maturity Treasury yields (DGS1MO to DGS30) are treated as par yields on a semiannual bond-equivalent basis. | `tenorwise/curve/fred.py`, `bootstrap.py` | The H.15 yields come from the Treasury's own fitted curve, not from individual traded bonds. Differences between particular issues (on/off-the-run, liquidity) are ignored. | 1 |
| A-03 | Tenors of 6 months and under are one payment with simple interest: DF = 1/(1 + y·T). | `bootstrap.py` | The short end follows a different compounding convention from the rest. On a flat 4% curve, the 1M and 3M zero rates are 3.3 bp and 2.0 bp above the level from 6 months on (calculated from the formulas). Kept by owner decision, because it follows how Treasury quotes bills (D-021). | 1 |
| A-04 | Zero rates are continuously compounded, linear in time between the 11 curve points, and flat before 1 month and beyond 30 years. | `bootstrap.py` (`ZeroCurve.zero_rate`) | Implied forward rates jump at each curve point. Cash flows beyond 30 years are discounted at the 30-year zero rate. | 1 |
| A-05 | The market data date is the latest date on which all 11 series have a value. Nothing is filled forward, and data more than 7 days older than the build fails the build. | `fred.py` (`select_par_curve`) | If one series is missing on the latest day, every tenor uses the earlier complete date. | 1 |
| A-06 | A rate shock is added to the 11 par yields, and the zero curve is rebuilt from scratch. | `tenorwise/curve/shocks.py` | A shock to one region's par yields also moves zero rates elsewhere through the bootstrap. On the 16 Nov 2026 curve, +25 bp on the 10-year tent lowers the 30-year zero rate by 13.6 bp (computed by the engine). | 1 |
| A-07 | Shocked par yields are floored at 0%, and the output flags when the floor binds. | `shocks.py` (`apply_shock`) | Large downward shocks on a low-rate curve are cut off at zero. Negative-rate scenarios cannot be represented. | 1 |
| A-08 | Key-rate shocks use straight-line tents at 2, 5, 10 and 30 years that sum to 1 at every tenor. | `shocks.py` (`tent_weight`) | Risk at maturities between the nodes is split between the two neighbouring buckets in straight-line proportion. | 1 |
| A-09 | Fixed-rate bonds pay half the annual coupon every six months and the face value at maturity, with payments at T, T−6 months, ... while positive. There are no call features, floating rates or default. | `tenorwise/instruments/bond.py` | Bonds that can be called early, or that might default, would be worth less than shown and have different risk. | 2 |
| A-10 | Each sector has one constant spread, added to the continuously compounded Treasury zero rate: DF = exp(−(z + s)·t). Spreads do not move when rates move. | `bond.py` (`dirty_price`) | Credit-spread risk is not modelled. Rate scenarios move Treasury rates only. With a spread s, the yield-based DV01 and the curve DV01 differ by about exp(s/2), roughly 0.5% at s = 1% (measured on a flat curve). | 2 |
| A-11 | Accrued interest grows in whole months: the semiannual coupon × (months elapsed in the current half-year) / 6. | `bond.py` (`accrued_interest`) | Accrued differs from day-count-based market accrued by up to a few days' interest (not measured). Dirty price is unaffected. | 2 |
| A-12 | The yield is compounded semiannually, including fractional periods: a cash flow at t years is discounted by (1 + y/2)^(−2t). | `tenorwise/analytics/yield_measures.py` | Matches QuantLib's convention under 30/360 (measured agreement 7e-16). It may differ slightly from street yield conventions for off-cycle bonds (not measured). | 2 |
| A-13 | Curve risk is measured by moving par yields and rebuilding the curve. Effective duration and convexity use ±25 bp parallel moves, DV01 uses ±1 bp, and key-rate DV01s use ±1 bp on each tent. The plan sets the first two sizes; the key-rate size is a design choice (D-025). | `tenorwise/analytics/risk.py` | On an upward-sloping curve, a 1 bp par move shifts long zero rates by more than 1 bp. Curve DV01 then exceeds yield-based DV01, by up to 4.45% for a 20-year zero bond on 16 Nov 2026 (measured). | 2 |
| A-14 | A mortgage pool is one level-payment fixed-rate loan with a gross coupon (WAC), a remaining term and an age. The investor's net coupon is WAC minus a 0.50 percentage point servicing and guarantee fee. | `tenorwise/instruments/mbs.py` | Real pools mix many loans with different rates, terms and borrowers. One average loan smooths their behaviour. STYLIZED. | 3 |
| A-15 | Prepayment speed follows a logistic S-curve in the refinancing incentive: CPR_min 6%, CPR_max 40%, k = 3, x0 = 1.0 percentage point, scaled by a ramp of min(1, age/30 months). The parameters are illustrative, not calibrated. | `mbs.py` (`PrepaymentModel`) | Every mortgage figure depends on these choices. Real speeds also depend on burnout, seasonality, house prices and loan size. STYLIZED. | 3 |
| A-16 | A scenario's mortgage rate is its 10-year par yield plus a fixed mortgage spread: OBMMIC30YF minus DGS10 on the latest date both exist, 2.14 pp on 16 Nov 2026. | `tenorwise/curve/mortgage_spread.py`, `mbs.py` | In real markets the spread widens and narrows with volatility and supply. Holding it fixed makes mortgage rates move one-for-one with the 10-year. | 3 |
| A-17 | Static valuation: cash flows are projected once under each scenario's curve and discounted. There is no rate volatility, no option-adjusted spread, no burnout and no seasonality. | `mbs.py` (`price`) | Understates the cost of the borrower's prepayment option compared with an OAS model. Can give negative durations for pools priced well above par. | 3 |
| A-18 | Pools are valued on a payment date (no accrued interest), with monthly cash flows at month/12 years, discounted at the Treasury zero rate plus a constant spread. | `mbs.py` (`price`) | Ignores the payment delay of real pass-throughs. Prices differ slightly from market conventions (not measured). | 3 |
| A-19 | The demonstration "current-coupon" pool has WAC equal to today's mortgage rate, is 36 months old with 324 months left, and is discounted at the spread that prices it at par (162.1 bp on 16 Nov 2026). | `tenorwise/analytics/mbs_profile.py`, tests | The discount spread is a calibration choice, not a market OAS. The pool is an example, not a holding. | 3 |
| A-20 | The portfolio's mix copies BNY's investment securities (10-Q, 30 Sept 2026) mapped into four sectors. Non-US government and CLOs are excluded. | `tenorwise/portfolio/sources.py` | The model omits 27.2% of BNY's securities, including its floating-rate CLOs (which have little rate risk) and foreign government bonds. | 4 |
| A-21 | BNY's weights combine AFS at fair value with HTM at amortized cost, and are applied as the model's amortized cost (D-038). | `sources.py`, `positions.py` | Sector weights by amortized cost differ slightly from what BNY's amortized-cost-only weights would be (AFS fair value is 2.4% below its amortized cost). | 4 |
| A-22 | Every position was bought at par (coupon = book yield). Bonds have whole half-years to maturity. Book yields are BNY's disclosed yield for the line and maturity bucket. Mortgage pool shares are solved to match BNY's line yields (D-036). | `data/portfolio/positions.json`, `positions.py` | No premium or discount purchase history. Real securities within a bucket have a spread of yields and maturities. | 4 |
| A-23 | The IG corporates sleeve is a proxy. Foreign covered bonds, non-agency MBS, other ABS and other debt are modelled as US-dollar fixed-rate bullets with a 100 bp spread. | `positions.json`, `valuation.py` | Ignores currency, amortization, prepayment and structure in those holdings. Labelled "proxy" throughout. | 4 |
| A-24 | Agency commercial MBS are modelled as fixed-rate bullets in the agency debt sector, with no prepayment. | `positions.json` | Reasonable for loans with prepayment penalties. It overstates their duration if prepayments occur (not measured). | 4 |
| A-25 | Sector spreads: Treasuries 0, agency mortgage calibrated per build (162.1 bp on 16 Nov 2026), agency debt 25 bp and IG proxy 100 bp (illustrative). All are held constant in every scenario (D-037). | `tenorwise/portfolio/valuation.py` | No credit or spread risk. The single mortgage spread over-discounts low-coupon pools. | 4 |
| A-26 | Amortized cost is the remaining cash flows discounted at the book yield. There are no hedge-accounting basis adjustments, no credit allowance and no taxes; AOCI effects are pre-tax. | `positions.py` | Unrealized losses are not comparable to BNY's reported figures (D-039). | 4 |
| A-27 | Scenarios are instantaneous shocks to the 11 par yields; the curve then holds still. Sector spreads and the mortgage spread do not move. | `tenorwise/scenarios/revaluation.py` | No path, no time passing, no spread widening. Real stress usually widens credit and mortgage spreads too, which would add losses (not modelled). | 5 |
| A-28 | The steepener and flattener are the key-rate tents scaled by 25, 50, 75 and 100 bp, giving straight lines between 2, 5, 10 and 30 years. | `tenorwise/scenarios/definitions.py` | One illustrative shape for each. | 5 |
| A-29 | The 2022 replay applies each par yield's change from 31 Dec 2021 to 30 Dec 2022 (FRED) to today's curve. | `tenorwise/scenarios/replay.py` | It assumes the same moves in basis points regardless of today's starting level. | 5 |
| A-30 | 12-month carry is gross interest income: amortized cost × book yield over 12 months (prorated if a bond matures sooner). Mortgage balances run down with the scenario's prepayments. There is no funding cost and no reinvestment of cash received. | `tenorwise/scenarios/carry.py` | Carry is not net interest income: it ignores deposit and funding costs, and the income from reinvesting maturing bonds and prepayments at new rates. | 5 |
| A-31 | AOCI impact is the change in AFS value, pre-tax. The HTM change is reported separately as not recognized. | `revaluation.py` | No tax effect, and no hedge accounting until Phase 6. | 5 |
